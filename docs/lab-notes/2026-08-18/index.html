Scale factor i: the imaginary continuation of yesterday's Ricci calculation turns sin into sinh — and reveals the −12 was a negative cosmological constant

Derivation, no campaign; the follow-up Chris requested to yesterday's Ricci-scalar check: repeat the same calculation with the turnaround scale factor 1 replaced by i = √−1. New chart script plots/plot_ricci_sinh.py (commit d77f944).

  • “Scale factor i” means sinh. Yesterday's flat cosmos was the A = 1 member of the family a(t) = A sin(t/A), which turns around at t = Aπ/2 with a = A. Setting A = i gives i sin(t/i) = sinh t — a real, monotonically growing scale factor. The turnaround has moved to imaginary time t = iπ/2, where a = sinh(iπ/2) = i: exactly Chris's phrasing. The Bang at t = 0 survives; the Crunch does not — the closed loop opens into a half-infinite, forever-expanding history.
  • The closed form flips one sign. For flat (d+1)-dimensional FRW the family gives R = d(d−1)/a² − d(d+1)/A², so A = 1 → 6/a² − 12 becomes A = i → R = 6/a² + 12 in 3+1 (2/a² + 6 in 2+1): positive everywhere, no sign change, settling onto the de Sitter value R = 12 as a → ∞. At the complex turnaround R = −6/A² = +6 (yesterday: −6); in general the meeting value −2d becomes +2d.
  • Physical reading: the constant term is a cosmological constant, and i flips its sign. Via the trace, the −12/A² term is RΛ = 4Λ with Λ = −3/A². So Chris's original sin universe is precisely the flat cosmos with Λ = −3 plus a curvature-like w = −1/3 fluid (ρ ∝ 1/a²) — its recollapse is driven by negative Λ — and the i-continuation keeps the fluid unchanged while flipping to Λ = +3: eternal inflation instead of a Crunch. Recollapse and inflation are Wick partners (sinh t = −i sin(it)).
  • The other two curves continue just as cleanly. The model-convention conformal reading a(z) = sin z → sinh z flips R = −6/a² to R = +6/a² in 3+1 (an exact mirror), though in 2+1 a single sign change survives: R = 2(sinh²z − 1)/a⁴, negative until z = ln(1+√2) ≈ 0.881. And the closed-sphere comparison a = 2 sin(t/2), k = +1 continues (A → 2i, k → −1) to a = 2 sinh(t/2), k = −1 — which is literally de Sitter space in its open-slicing chart: R = 3 constant (3/2 in 2+1). Yesterday's “R ≥ 0 sphere” becomes constant positive curvature exactly.
  • Consistency with yesterday's invariant. The convention-free fact was flat + turnaround ⇒ R < 0 at the top. The continued universe evades it honestly: with A imaginary the turnaround condition cos(t/A) = 0 has no real solution, so nothing forces R negative — and indeed it never is (in the 3+1 readings). Neither reading hosts the model's closed z ∈ (0, π) loop (there is no Crunch to close onto), so like yesterday this is an interpretation result, not a change to the model.

Takeaway for Chris: replacing the turnaround scale factor 1 by i replaces sin by sinh, and every formula continues analytically — R = 6/a² − 12 becomes R = 6/a² + 12. The deeper point the continuation exposes is what the constant −12 was: a negative cosmological constant Λ = −3. The sign-changing Ricci scalar of the cosmic-time sin universe, and its recollapse, are both the signature of Λ < 0; multiplying the scale factor by i is exactly the Λ → −Λ switch.
